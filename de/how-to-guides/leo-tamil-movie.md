# Leo (2023): A Tamil Action Thriller

*Leo* is a 2023 Indian Tamil-language action thriller directed by Lokesh Kanagaraj. The film stars Vijay as Parthiban, alongside Trisha Krishnan, Sanjay Dutt, and Arjun.

## At a glance

- **Release date:** 19 October 2023
- **Director:** Lokesh Kanagaraj
- **Music:** Anirudh Ravichander
- **Production:** Seven Screen Studio
- **Genre:** Action thriller

## Story

Parthiban is a quiet café owner in Himachal Pradesh who lives with his family. After a violent incident draws public attention, people from his past begin to question who he really is. The film follows the tension that unfolds as he tries to protect his family and keep his life from being turned upside down.

## Cast

- Vijay as Parthiban / Leo
- Trisha Krishnan as Sathya
- Sanjay Dutt as Antony Das
- Arjun as Harold Das

*Leo* is part of Lokesh Kanagaraj's interconnected film universe, often referred to as the Lokesh Cinematic Universe (LCU).
