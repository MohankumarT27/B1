# The Nohara Family Home

![Colorful guide illustration](images/shinchan-guide-art.svg)

The Nohara home is one of the series' central settings. Everyday routines—getting ready, eating together, tidying up, and relaxing—often provide the setup for Shinchan's jokes.

| Daily routine | How it appears in the stories |
|---|---|
| Morning preparations | The family tries to get the day started |
| Meals | Food and family conversations become comic moments |
| Chores | Shinchan's interpretation of a task can complicate it |
| Family time | The household shares outings and quiet moments |

## A familiar place

Because the home is recognizable and lived-in, its small details help make the family's adventures relatable to viewers.
