# The Nohara Family

![Colorful guide illustration](images/shinchan-guide-art.svg)

The Nohara family is at the heart of Shinchan's everyday stories. Their affectionate but often chaotic home life gives the series many of its most familiar comic situations.

| Family member | Role in the household |
|---|---|
| Hiroshi | Shinchan's father, balancing work and family |
| Misae | Shinchan's mother, keeping the household running |
| Shinchan | The energetic older child |
| Himawari | Shinchan's younger sister |
| Shiro | The family's loyal dog |

## Family dynamics

The stories often pair Shinchan's antics with his parents' practical worries. Small errands, meals, and family outings become opportunities for jokes as well as warm moments together.
