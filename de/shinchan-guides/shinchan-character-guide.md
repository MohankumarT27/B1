# Shinchan Character Guide

![Colorful guide illustration](../../assets/documentation/shinchan-guide-art.svg)

Shinnosuke Nohara, better known as Shinchan, is the mischievous young star of Yoshito Usui's comedy manga and its animated adaptation. His curiosity and confidence turn ordinary family and neighborhood moments into comic adventures.

| Detail | Overview |
|---|---|
| Full name | Shinnosuke Nohara |
| Common name | Shinchan |
| Age in the series | Five years old |
| Home | Kasukabe, Japan |
| Main genres | Comedy and family |

## What makes him memorable

Shinchan is outspoken, imaginative, and often unintentionally troublesome. The contrast between his bold behavior and the everyday concerns of the adults around him is a recurring source of humor.
