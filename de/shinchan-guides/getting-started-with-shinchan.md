# Getting Started with Shinchan

![Colorful guide illustration](../../assets/documentation/shinchan-guide-art.svg)

New viewers can begin with episodes or stories centered on the Nohara family, Shinchan's kindergarten friends, or everyday adventures in Kasukabe. The series is generally episodic, so many stories can be enjoyed on their own.

| If you enjoy… | Try stories about… |
|---|---|
| Family comedy | The Noharas at home or on an outing |
| School adventures | Shinchan and his kindergarten classmates |
| Everyday humor | Errands and neighborhood encounters |
| Bigger adventures | The feature-length animated films |

## A simple viewing approach

Start with a story that interests you, get to know the recurring cast, and explore other formats afterward. Local availability and episode ordering can vary by region and platform.
