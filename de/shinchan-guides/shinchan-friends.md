# Shinchan's Friends

![Colorful guide illustration](images/shinchan-guide-art.svg)

Shinchan's friends add different personalities and points of view to his adventures. Their playtime and kindergarten experiences create a lively picture of childhood friendship.

| Friend | A memorable characteristic |
|---|---|
| Kazama | Studious and sometimes image-conscious |
| Nene | Confident and fond of pretend play |
| Masao | Gentle and easily worried |
| Bo | Quiet, observant, and distinctive |

## Friendship in the series

The group may bicker, compete, or misunderstand one another, but they continue to share games and adventures. Their differences are a steady source of playful comedy.
